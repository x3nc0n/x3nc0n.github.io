LingoPop — iOS

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Overview

LingoPop ("the App") is an offline iOS puzzle game designed for word and language play. This privacy policy explains how the App handles data.

Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties.

The App:

  • Does NOT transmit any data off your device
  • Does NOT require online accounts or registration
  • Does NOT send analytics or usage data to any server
  • Does NOT contain advertising or third-party trackers
  • Does NOT request permission to access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health data

Local Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device only to enable gameplay:

  • Game progress and save state: In-progress puzzles and completed games
  • User preferences: Display settings and game options you choose
  • Statistics and outcomes: Your scores, completion history, and game results

The developer does not collect or transmit this data; it is stored only in the app's private sandbox on your device. Uninstalling the app removes LingoPop's own copy of this data, though device-level or cloud backups you have enabled (see "Data Deletion" below) are managed by the platform, not by LingoPop.

System Sharing

LingoPop includes an optional feature to share game results with other apps or services (e.g., Messages, Mail, social media) through the system share sheet.

How this works:

  • You may select a result to share and choose a destination (e.g., Messages, Mail)
  • Only the text you select is sent to that destination
  • LingoPop does not receive, store, or process the shared content after you send it
  • Sharing is entirely optional; you can use the App without sharing anything

Permissions

LingoPop does not request any device permissions. The app operates entirely within its sandbox and does not access microphone, camera, location, contacts, photo library, or other sensitive device features.

Third-Party Services

LingoPop does not integrate with any third-party services for analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud storage.

Children's Privacy

LingoPop is designed for a general audience, including children. The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data from any user, including children, and no online account creation is required.

  • No data is collected from children and transmitted off the device
  • No online account creation required
  • All data remains stored locally on the device

This description reflects the App's technical behavior and is not a legal compliance determination; if you have questions about how this applies to a specific law or jurisdiction, please consult your own legal advisor.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes LingoPop's own local data (game progress, preferences, and statistics) from its app sandbox. LingoPop does not itself back up, sync, or retain this data elsewhere. However, LingoPop does not control your device's operating system or cloud services — for example, if iCloud/device backups are enabled, a copy of the app's sandboxed data may exist in those backups until you delete it there or turn off backups for the app. No data removal tool is currently built into the app; future versions may include an in-app reset option.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the LingoPop app, please visit:
https://www.spaid.dev/support

Sight Word Phrases — Android

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

Overview

Sight Word Phrases ("the App") is an Android application designed to help children practice reading. This privacy policy explains how the App handles data.

Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or share any data with third parties.

The App:

  • Does NOT transmit any data off your device
  • Does NOT require online accounts or registration
  • Does NOT send analytics or usage data to any server
  • Does NOT contain advertising or third-party trackers
  • Does NOT connect to the internet

Local Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device only to provide its features:

  • User profiles: A name (chosen by the user) to identify readers and track their progress separately
  • Reading session history: Which word list level was practiced and when
  • Phrase attempt results: Performance data for each phrase (words missed, time taken, completion status)

The developer does not collect or transmit this data; it is stored only in the app's private database on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the App's own copy of this data, though device-level or cloud backups you have enabled (see "Your Rights" below) are managed by the platform, not by this App.

Permissions

Microphone Permission

The App requests microphone permission to enable speech recognition for reading practice.

How we use it:

  • Audio is processed locally on your device using offline speech recognition
  • No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted
  • Speech recognition happens entirely offline
  • Audio processing stops when you exit the app

Storage Permission

The App extracts a bundled speech recognition model (~40MB) to your device's storage for offline use.

How we use it:

  • The model is included with the app and extracted from app assets on first launch — no download is required
  • Model file is stored in the app's private directory
  • Model is removed when you uninstall the app

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for children. The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data from any user, including children, and no online account creation is required.

  • No data is collected from children or transmitted off the device
  • No online account creation required
  • User profiles are stored locally only and contain only a chosen display name

This description reflects the App's technical behavior and is not a legal compliance determination; if you have questions about how this applies to a specific law or jurisdiction, please consult your own legal advisor.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following open-source libraries:

  • Vosk Speech Recognition: Offline speech recognition engine (processes audio locally, bundled with the app)
  • Android Jetpack / Room: Local on-device database for storing reading progress

These libraries operate entirely on your device and do not collect or transmit data.

Data Security

All data is stored locally in the app's private storage, accessible only to the App. No data is transmitted over the internet, so there is no risk of interception or remote breach.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in this document with a new "Last Updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy for Sight Word Phrases, please contact:
https://www.spaid.dev/support

Your Rights

Since no data leaves your device:

  • All your data is under your control on your own device
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to permanently remove the App's own local data
  • The App does not itself back up, sync, or retain this data elsewhere. However, the App does not control your device's operating system or cloud services — for example, if device backups are enabled, a copy of the App's data may exist in those backups until you delete it there or turn off backups for the app.

Consent

By using Sight Word Phrases, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
